# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone.

Be respectful, assume good intent, focus criticism on ideas and code, and make room for people with different backgrounds and experience levels. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not tolerated.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This concise policy follows the principles of the Contributor Covenant.
